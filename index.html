Shrink

Make a photo or PDF small enough to send.

How it works

  • Pick a photo or PDF.
  • Pick WhatsApp (2 MB) or Email (5 MB).
  • Tap Shrink, then share the smaller copy.

Free and Pro

  • Free: three shrinks a day.
  • Shrink Pro (optional) unlocks unlimited shrinks, PDFs, batch up to 20 and a custom target size. It is sold two ways, and both unlock exactly the same things:
    • Lifetime — €3.99, a one-time purchase that never renews.
    • Monthly — €0.99 a month, auto-renewing, with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers.
    Prices are shown in your local currency in the app and may differ outside the euro area.
  • Already bought it? Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in Settings.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?
On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Shrink and tap Cancel Subscription, or tap Manage Subscription in Shrink's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next month. If you cancel during the free trial you are not charged. Payment and refunds are handled by Apple.

Common questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?
No. Shrink works entirely on your iPhone. There is no server and no account.

It says it cannot reach the target.
Some files cannot get that small without becoming unusable. Shrink tells you the smallest usable size rather than handing back a file that is still too big.

My PDF came back unchanged.
It was already under the target, so Shrink left it alone.
