Privacy Policy

Shrink collects nothing. This page explains what that means in practice.

What Shrink collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Accounts

There are no accounts. You never give Shrink an email address, a name or a password.

Your photos and PDFs

  • Files are read and compressed on your iPhone.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Shrink has no server to upload to.
  • The original file is never modified — Shrink works on a copy.
  • Working copies are kept in the app's cache and deleted when the app moves to the background.
  • You can delete them yourself at any time: Settings → Delete local files.

Purchases

Shrink Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase handled entirely by Apple. Shrink asks Apple whether this Apple Account owns it. Payment details are never seen by Shrink or by its developer.

Children

Shrink is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
